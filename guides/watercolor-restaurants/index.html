WaterColor restaurants

WaterColor sits between Grayton Beach and Seaside, and the dining list is short enough to decide from. You can do breakfast in town and a dressed-up dinner without leaving the community.

Breakfast is Beach Happy Cafe, Scratch Biscuit Kitchen, and The Perfect Pig. For the evening, add Fish Out of Water, Pizza by the Sea, and The Wine Bar, plus The Perfect Pig if you want a place that covers both.

Fish Out of Water is the waterfront restaurant at the WaterColor Inn — the dressed-up seafood meal, and one of Lauren’s Favorites. Beach Happy Cafe is where you get coffee.

If you’re staying at the inn, Fish Out of Water is the on-property choice. Scratch Biscuit Kitchen, Beach Happy Cafe, and Pizza by the Sea are the more casual stops. The Wine Bar is the later glass. Breakfast isn’t an all-day service, so check the hours on the card before you walk over.

Common questions

Is WaterColor only a breakfast stop?

No. Dinner is Fish Out of Water, Pizza by the Sea, The Perfect Pig, and The Wine Bar. Breakfast is Beach Happy Cafe, Scratch Biscuit Kitchen, and The Perfect Pig. Show WaterColor breakfast.
