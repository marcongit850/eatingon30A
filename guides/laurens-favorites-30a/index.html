Lauren’s Favorites on 30A

When you don’t want to sort the whole highway, start with Lauren’s Favorites. They’re her short list: Mimmo’s 30A in Blue Mountain Beach, Fish Out of Water in WaterColor, Old Florida Fish House in Seagrove Beach, Surfing Deer in Seagrove Beach, Raw & Juicy in Alys Beach, and Pescado Seafood Grill & Rooftop Bar in Rosemary Beach.

The seafood meals are Fish Out of Water, Old Florida Fish House, Surfing Deer, and Pescado Seafood Grill & Rooftop Bar. Mimmo’s 30A is Italian in Blue Mountain Beach and Raw & Juicy is the cafe stop in Alys Beach. In Seagrove Beach, Old Florida Fish House and Surfing Deer are both on the list.

Mimmo’s 30A, Fish Out of Water, Old Florida Fish House, and Raw & Juicy are kid friendly. Surfing Deer and Pescado Seafood Grill & Rooftop Bar aren’t.

This is a short list, not a ranking of every good meal on 30A. Hours live on the card. If you want more in the same town, open that town’s page after you’ve looked at Lauren’s pick.
