Rosemary Beach

Rosemary Beach restaurants

Rosemary Beach is the cobblestone town just east of Alys Beach, and it’s an easy place to park once and walk to dinner. The restaurants here cover morning cafes, a real dinner selection, and seafood if that’s the meal you came for.

For breakfast, look at 3rd Cup, Amavida Coffee Roasters, Charlie’s Café, Cowgirl Kitchen, and The Summer Kitchen Café. Seafood is Crabby Steve’s, Gallion’s, Havana Beach Bar & Grill, Pescado Seafood Grill & Rooftop Bar, and Restaurant Paradis. Crabby Steve’s is up in High Pointe; the others are in town.

Pescado, the rooftop, is one of Lauren’s Favorites if you want a shorter shortlist inside town. The town page is where to read about Rosemary Beach itself; this page is for choosing the meal.

The streets are cobblestone, so real shoes beat beach flip-flops after dark. Hours move with the season. Rosemary isn’t a stroll from Seaside or WaterColor — drive over, park once, and stay for the evening.

Common questions

What can I eat in Rosemary Beach?

Breakfast at the cafes, dinner through the evening, and seafood at Crabby Steve’s, Gallion’s, Havana Beach Bar & Grill, Pescado Seafood Grill & Rooftop Bar, and Restaurant Paradis. Show Rosemary Beach seafood.

Is this the same list as the town page?

Same restaurants. The town page tells you about Rosemary Beach. Here you can open the directory or the map with the town already selected. See them in the directory.

Where does Rosemary Beach sit on 30A?

Just east of Alys Beach and west of Inlet Beach. It’s a drive from Seaside, not a walk, and it’s the cobblestone town if you want to park once for the evening. See the walkable towns.
