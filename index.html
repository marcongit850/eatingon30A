A directory for the whole corridor

Eating on 30A is a guide to restaurants along Scenic Highway 30A in Walton County, Florida. From casual beachside bites and fresh Gulf seafood to upscale dining and local favorites, the directory is meant to help you find a place by town, meal, or cuisine. Browse Dune Allen, Gulf Place, Blue Mountain, Grayton Beach, WaterColor, Seaside, Seagrove, Seacrest, Watersound, Alys Beach, Rosemary Beach, Inlet Beach, and Watersound Origins.
