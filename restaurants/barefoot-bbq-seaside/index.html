Barefoot BBQ is a laid-back, beachy spot serving up big flavor in a fun, outdoor setting that's family and dog friendly. Known for high-quality, pecan-smoked meats, Barefoot BBQ also makes nearly everything in-house, from the cheese used in their mac & cheese and nachos to the potato salad, baked beans, coleslaw, and smoked tuna dip.

They're also known for their popular Bushwackers, 30A Frosé, and Bloody Marys, making it a great stop whether you're coming for a full meal or just a drink. As their tagline says: Barefoot BBQ, It'll Knock Your Socks Off!
