Proper Restaurant & Bar is in 30Avenue in Inlet Beach, across from the green space. There is a covered patio, a kids menu, and lawn games.

Happy hour runs daily from 4 to 6p, with wines by the glass, select drafts, specialty cocktails, and food. Weekend brunch is 10a to 2p, with live jazz from 11a to 2p. Friday and Saturday DJ parties start at 10PM.
