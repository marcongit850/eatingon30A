Pazzo Italiano serves Italian food in Nearby near Scenic Highway 30A, on Hwy 393. Come by for lunch and dinner. The listing includes reservations. It’s marked kid friendly.

This is the Santa Rosa Beach Pazzo, not the Destin restaurant. Alfonso Di Bonito, a pizza chef from Napoli, and Levente Tischler run the kitchen, and the pies come out of a wood-fired oven.
