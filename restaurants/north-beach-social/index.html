North Beach Social serves Southern food in Nearby near Scenic Highway 30A, on Hwy 331. Come by for lunch and dinner. The listing includes outdoor dining, live music, and happy hour. It’s marked kid friendly.

North Beach Social is the open-air bar downstairs from Farm & Fire, at the south end of the 331 bridge. The deck faces the sunset over Choctawhatchee Bay, with a stretch of white sand out front. Locals order Grits a Ya Ya and poke bowls, and the staff lights a bonfire at night when the weather allows.
